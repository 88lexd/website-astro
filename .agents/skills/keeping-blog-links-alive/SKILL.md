## Keeping links alive
This repository is powered by Astro and the blog contents in located in `contents/blog/` directory.

There may be links in old blog posts pointing to this repository "https://github.com/88lexd/website-astro"

As the directory structure may change over time, you are to check all previous blog posts for any hyperlinks that needs to be updated to ensure they are not broken after the change is merged into the default/main branch.

You are to only check for broken links related to THIS repository only.
